# Privacy Policy for MoneyBag

**Effective Date: May 2, 2026**

MoneyBag ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the MoneyBag mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

*   **Personal Information:** If you choose to use the "Google Sync" feature, we collect your Google account email address and name to identify your backup data in Firebase.
*   **Financial Data:** The app processes your transaction history, account balances, and categories. By default, this data is stored **locally** on your device. If you use Google Sync, this data is encrypted and stored in our secure Firebase database.
*   **App Activity:** We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous information about how the app is used (e.g., which features are used most) to help us improve the experience.
*   **Advertising IDs:** We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

*   To provide and maintain the service (including Cloud Sync and PC Access).
*   To improve and personalize your experience.
*   To show relevant advertisements.
*   To notify you about changes to our service.

## 3. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We use industry-standard encryption for data synced to the cloud. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 4. Your Choices

*   **Local Storage:** You can use the app without ever signing in to Google. In this case, no personal or financial data leaves your device.
*   **Deletion:** You can delete your transactions or accounts at any time.
